AstroBaki, latex wiki for astronomy lectures

09 September 2010

This project grew as an off-shoot of my efforts to latex lecture notes on-the-fly for classes I took as a grad student here. The various professors involved have agreed with my efforts to place these notes into the public domain for editing, revision, and addition. The cool (but still experimental) aspect of the wiki is that the content can be written in pure LaTeX (inside of tags), and a separate plugin under the hood interprets that content into the traditional MediaWiki syntax. That means you can collaboratively write latex-based papers in full LaTeX glory that compile on their own, and if you paste that content into the wiki, it will be rendered into HTML (although only a subset of that LaTeX markup actually gets rendered visually for viewing--the rest is just ignored). - Aaron Parsons
